Hand Drone XS

A 3D endless runner you control with your hand. Your webcam tracks real-time hand position, and the drone responds directly in the browser.

Play Hand Drone XS

What it is

Hand Drone XS is a browser game built with Three.js for 3D graphics and MediaPipe Tasks Vision for hand tracking. Instead of a keyboard, gamepad, or touchscreen joystick, you steer the drone by moving your hand in front of the webcam.

How it plays

  • Move your hand left or right to steer.
  • Move your hand up or down to change altitude.
  • Fly through rings to increase your score.
  • Avoid trees while the game speed increases.
  • Your best score is stored locally in the browser.

Requirements

The game needs a modern browser, camera permission, and an internet connection for its external Three.js and MediaPipe resources. Camera video is used by the in-browser hand-tracking runtime.

Technology

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Three.js, MediaPipe Tasks Vision, Web Audio, and browser camera APIs.
